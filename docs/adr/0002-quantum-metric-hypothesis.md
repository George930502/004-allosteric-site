# 0002 — Working hypothesis for the quantum metric

**Status:** proposed · 2026-08-19 · *to be accepted, revised or superseded in Phase 2*

## Context

The challenge explicitly leaves the quantum metric to participants: "Participants are
free to hypothesize and define the specific quantum metric that serves as the proxy
for this biological signal" (`CHALLENGE.md` §4.1). The metric choice is the scientific
core of the submission, so it is recorded as a hypothesis now and tested, not assumed.

Assumed background: the elastic network hypothesis (C6) — contact topology is the
primary driver of signal propagation, so the object we compute on is a graph derived
from the apo structure.

## Hypothesis

Model allosteric communication as **single-excitation quantum transport on the residue
contact network**. With one qubit per node and an XY-type coupling Hamiltonian
restricted to the single-excitation sector, the dynamics reduce to a continuous-time
quantum walk generated by the network Hamiltonian `H` (adjacency, Laplacian, or an
ENM-Hessian-derived weighting — an ablation, not a settled choice).

Inject an excitation at the active site `s` and evolve `|ψ(t)> = exp(-iHt)|s>`. Define
the connectivity between residues `i` and `j` from the time-averaged transfer
probability

```
C_ij  =  lim_{T→∞} (1/T) ∫_0^T |<j| exp(-iHt) |i>|^2 dt
```

with the ranking score for residue `i` being `C_{i,active site}`, corrected for the
trivial degree/locality bias.

**Why this could beat the classical analog.** Classical diffusion on the same graph,
`exp(-Lt)`, is a positive, memoryless, monotone smoothing process: contributions from
distinct structural paths always add, so score falls off with topological distance and
distal specificity is washed out. Quantum evolution propagates *amplitudes*, which
carry phase, so contributions along different paths can interfere constructively or
destructively. Genuine allosteric channels are exactly the case where a distal site is
strongly coupled to the active site through a specific set of paths rather than through
bulk proximity — a signal an interference-sensitive, ballistic propagator can resolve
and a diffusive one cannot. This is also the property the challenge points at:
non-local correlations and interference as the claimed quantum advantage
(`CHALLENGE.md` §2, refs [10,11]).

## Candidate metrics to ablate in Phase 2

1. Time-averaged transfer probability (above) — the default.
2. Peak transfer probability over a bounded time window, and the time at which it
   occurs (a "signal arrival time" with a natural biological reading).
3. Integrated coherence / off-diagonal mass of the reduced state at the target residue.
4. Quantum Fisher information of the walk with respect to a perturbation at the
   candidate site — closer to the biological question "does binding here change what
   the active site feels?".
5. Perturbation formulation: compare propagation with and without an on-site energy
   shift at the candidate residue, i.e. simulate the *binding event* rather than only
   the resting communication. Physically the closest analog to allosteric inhibition,
   and likely the strongest variant if it survives the cost of N re-simulations.

## Known risks

- **Degree bias.** Transfer probability correlates with node degree and burial; without
  a null-model correction the method may rediscover "buried and well-connected", not
  "allosteric". A degree-preserving randomised-network null is required.
- **Localisation.** Disordered graphs can Anderson-localise the walk, killing distal
  transfer entirely. Watch for it on c-Myc especially.
- **Time-averaging washout.** The infinite-time average may discard the very transient
  structure that carries the signal; metric 2 exists as the hedge.
- **Resource cost.** One qubit per residue does not fit near-term hardware for these
  targets (C3) — this is what makes Phase 4 coarse-graining mandatory rather than a
  nice-to-have.

## Decision to be made in Phase 2

Accept one primary metric (with the others reported as ablations) on the basis of
enrichment statistics from the Phase 1 harness, not on aesthetics.
