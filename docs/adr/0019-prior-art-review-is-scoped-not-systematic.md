# 0019 — Prior-art review is scoped, not systematic

**Status:** accepted · 2026-08-21

## Context

The evidence note called itself systematic despite using one retrieval service, lacking a
registered protocol and screening ledger, and relying partly on unverified extraction-model
text. Its negative searches therefore cannot prove absence or a genuine gap.

## Decision

Choose option (b): relabel it a scoped evidence review and qualify every negative result as
“not retrieved by the recorded search.” It may guide source checking but cannot support a
systematic-review or absence-of-prior-art claim.

## Consequences

The stronger claims are unavailable. The cost is a narrower novelty statement. A systematic
claim is unblocked only by a registered protocol, complete database queries and dates,
deduplication, eligibility/exclusion ledger, duplicate screening, raw-source verification,
risk-of-bias assessment, and PRISMA-style flow.
