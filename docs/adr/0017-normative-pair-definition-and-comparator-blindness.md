# 0017 — One normative pair definition; blindness belongs to evaluation

**Status:** accepted · 2026-08-21

## Context

The benchmark described nine clauses as field consensus even though its site-apo,
scoreable-portion, second-site, state-disclosure, and procedural non-circularity choices are
partly or wholly repository policy. Comparator blindness was also mixed into pair validity,
although it depends on a method's training history rather than on the two structures.

## Decision

`docs/benchmark/README.md` is the normative operational definition. Every admission clause
must carry one or more authority tags: **IN-DOMAIN**, **BORROWED**, or **REPOSITORY POLICY**.
The evidence dossier may trace and quote sources but may not substitute a differently worded
definition. Comparator blindness is a separate evaluation disclosure, not a pair-admission
clause.

## Consequences

Local choices are reviewable as policy rather than misrepresented as consensus. Existing
pair admission does not change merely because the authority labels are corrected; clause
(v)'s executable biological-assembly defect is handled separately. Changing any normative
clause now requires amending this ADR and the verbatim definition in both benchmark documents.
